## DOWNLOAD SPECIFICATIONS

### Testimonial Images:
- Format: JPG
- Size: 400x400px minimum (will resize during download)
- Quality: High-resolution professional portraits
- Style: Business professional attire
- Diversity: Mix of genders and ethnicities
- Usage: Royalty-free from Unsplash

### Logo/Badge Images:
- Format: SVG preferred (PNG as fallback)
- Style: Clean, simple, professional
- Colors: Adaptable/neutral for theme integration
- Usage: Free commercial use from approved sources
- Quality: Vector-based for scalability

## ATTRIBUTION REQUIREMENTS

### Unsplash Photos:
All Unsplash photos require attribution in the format:
"Photo by [Photographer Name] on Unsplash"

### Icon Resources:
- Flaticon: Attribution may be required for free tier
- IconScout: Check license terms
- Reshot: No attribution required
- Noun Project: Attribution required for free tier
- SVG Repo: Generally no attribution required
