# GEO Content Guidelines - ARDA Astro Template

## 🎯 Overview

These guidelines implement Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) strategies for the ARDA Astro Template, ensuring content is optimized for AI-powered search engines and language models in 2025.

**Core Principle:** Content must be optimized for AI **citation** and **representation** in generated answers, not just traditional keyword rankings.

---

## 📝 Content Structure Standards

### Question-Answer Format Requirements

**Primary Structure Pattern:**
1. **Question-Based Subheading** (H2/H3 as natural question)
2. **Direct Answer** (First 75-100 words with immediate response)
3. **Supporting Details** (Context, examples, specifics)
4. **Authority Signals** (Stats, quotes, citations when relevant)

**Example Implementation:**
```markdown
## What Makes ARDA Different from Other Agencies?

ARDA combines technical expertise with strategic thinking to deliver web solutions that drive real business results. Unlike traditional agencies that focus only on design or development, we take a holistic approach that includes SEO optimization, performance enhancement, and ongoing strategic support.

Our process includes comprehensive discovery, user research, and post-launch optimization to ensure your investment continues to deliver value. We've helped over 50 businesses increase their online revenue by an average of 40% within the first six months.

*Supporting details and case studies follow...*
```

### Content Length Guidelines

- **Section Length:** 75-300 words per section
- **Paragraph Focus:** One main idea per paragraph
- **Sentence Length:** Maximum 20 words per sentence
- **Answer Sections:** Direct answer in first 75-100 words

### Semantic Signal Words

**Include these context cues to help AI systems:**
- "In summary" / "To summarize"
- "The most important" / "Key point"
- "Step 1" / "First" / "Next"
- "Common mistake" / "Best practice"
- "For example" / "Specifically"

## 🔤 Writing Style & Voice

### Conversational Tone Requirements

**Write as if responding to someone directly:**
- Use "you" and "your" frequently
- Ask rhetorical questions to engage
- Use contractions when natural ("we'll" vs "we will")
- Avoid overly formal or robotic language
- Sound like a knowledgeable friend explaining concepts

**Example Transformation:**
```markdown
<!-- OLD: Formal, keyword-focused -->
ARDA provides comprehensive web development services utilizing modern frameworks and optimization techniques to deliver high-performance solutions.

<!-- NEW: Conversational, answer-focused -->
Looking for a web development partner who actually understands your business goals? We create fast, user-friendly websites that help you attract more customers and grow your revenue.
```

### Authority & Credibility Integration

**Include these elements in content:**
- **Statistics with sources:** "Studies show..." or "According to..."
- **Specific metrics:** "40% increase" instead of "significant improvement"
- **Expert insights:** Brief quotes or references to industry leaders
- **Case study references:** Real results from actual projects
- **Timeframes:** "Within 6 months" instead of "quickly"

## 🔍 SEO Integration Guidelines

### Meta Tag Standards

**Meta Descriptions (155-160 characters):**
- Start with a question or direct benefit
- Include conversational language
- Answer a specific user intent
- Include a call-to-action when appropriate

**Example:**
```html
<!-- OLD -->
<meta name="description" content="ARDA web development services, custom solutions, responsive design">

<!-- NEW -->
<meta name="description" content="Need a website that actually grows your business? ARDA creates high-performance sites that convert visitors into customers. Get your free strategy session.">
```

**Title Tags (Under 60 characters):**
- Focus on user intent, not just keywords
- Use natural, conversational phrasing
- Include the current year (2025) when relevant
- Target question-based searches

### Internal Linking Strategy

**Link with descriptive, conversational anchor text:**
```markdown
<!-- OLD -->
Learn more about our [services].

<!-- NEW -->
Curious about [how we help businesses grow through strategic web development]?
```

## 📱 Responsive & Accessible Writing

### Mobile-First Content Structure

**Optimize for voice search and mobile reading:**
- Short paragraphs (2-3 sentences maximum)
- Bullet points and numbered lists
- Clear subheadings every 150-200 words
- Scannable format with white space

### Question Variations for Voice Search

**Include natural question variations:**
- "What is..." / "How do I..." / "Why should I..."
- "Can you help me..." / "What's the best way to..."
- "How much does..." / "How long does it take..."
